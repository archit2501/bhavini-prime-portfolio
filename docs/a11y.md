# Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA audit

Target: WCAG 2.2 level AA (decision Q9). Audited 20 October 2026 on the five Playwright devices (iPhone 15, Pixel 8, iPad gen 11, 1280 and 1440 desktops), Chromium and WebKit.

| axe, tags `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a`, `wcag21aa`, `wcag22aa` | `tests/e2e/a11y.spec.ts`                                     | All 32 routes the site builds, on all five devices                                                    |
| axe on open overlays                                             | `preview-card`, `search`, `player`, `intro` and `a11y` specs | Hover card, search panel, profile menu, "Behind the scenes" panel, "Who's visiting?" picker           |

## Keyboard walkthrough

Recorded by tabbing through each screen with no mouse. The ring is the same everywhere: a 3px white outline, 2px off, with a 6px dark halo so it shows on light art too (`src/styles/global.css`). `scroll-padding-top` keeps a focused element clear of the fixed nav.

### Every page

1. **Skip to content** is the first stop. It slides in above the nav and moves focus to `<main>`.
2. **Nav:**
   - The wordmark (home), then Home, Experience, Education, Skills, Career Guide and Contact.
   - Below 1280px those links fold into **Menu**, a popover. Esc closes it.
   - Then **Search**, **Browse all titles**, **My List** and the **Profile menu** button.
3. **Search** opens the frosted panel with the cursor in the field.
   - Down moves into the results; the arrow keys move between them, and Home and End jump to the first and last.
   - Up from the first result returns to the field, and Enter opens a result.
   - Esc closes the panel and puts focus back on the Search button.
4. **Profile menu:**
   - Contact, Download resume, LinkedIn, **Switch profile**, then the four profiles.
   - Each profile button is a toggle (`aria-pressed`).
   - Picking one closes the menu and returns focus to the avatar, whose name says who is visiting.
5. **Footer:** LinkedIn, F1Jobs.io, Email, Resume (PDF) and Back to top.

### "Who's visiting?" and the ident

- **The picker:**
  - On the first home visit it opens as a modal dialog with focus on **Recruiter**.
  - Tab moves through the four profiles and **Skip for now**; Esc dismisses it.
  - The page behind is inert until it's answered.
- **The ident:**
  - A modal dialog with focus on **Skip**. Esc also skips it, and skipping silences the flourish.
  - Afterwards focus goes to the avatar.

### Home

1. **Hero:**
   - The title lettering (a link), **Episode 1 / Watch now**, **Add to My List** (a toggle) and **More details**.
   - Then Previous title, Next title, **Pause the carousel** and one button per slide.
   - Hidden slides are `inert`, so Tab only ever reaches the visible one.
   - The carousel pauses while focus is inside it and never advances under reduced motion.
2. **Rows:**
   - Each card is one stop.
   - Focusing a card opens the hover card after the usual delay. Tab walks through the hover card's own controls (Watch now, My List, details) and then on to the next card.
   - Esc closes the hover card and returns focus to its card.
   - The row's back and forward buttons follow the cards. "See more" sits at the row heading.
3. **Top 10:** each link reads as "Number 1: $100K+ in revenue F1Jobs.io", and so on.

### Title pages

1. Watch trailer (a toggle), Add to My List, Like, Not for me (one choice), Share, then the white **Watch now** CTA.
2. The skill links, the cast link (to Contact), and for films the Prequel or Sequel link.
3. **The tab list:**
   - One stop. Left and Right (Home, End) move between Episodes, Related and Details with a roving tabindex. The hash follows, and Tab goes into the panel.
4. Episodes (each still plays that episode), Related cards, "Recruiters also viewed" and Trailers.

- **Trailer controls:** Esc stops the immersive trailer, and Watch trailer toggles it.

### Watch page (player)

- **Tab order:**
  - Subtitles, Volume (disabled: the trailers have no sound), Full screen and Close player.
  - Then Back 10, Play/Pause, Forward 10, the **Seek** slider (its value is spoken, e.g. "4 seconds of 12 seconds"), **View all** and **Skip intro**.
- **Shortcuts:**
  - Space or k plays and pauses; Left and Right (or j and l) skip 10 s.
  - f is full screen, m says there's no sound, and c toggles subtitles.
  - Esc closes the player, unless it's full screen, where Esc leaves full screen first.
  - Buttons and the slider keep their own keys.
- **The chrome:**
  - It fades after 3 s of playback but never while keyboard focus is in it.
  - Any key brings it back. When hidden it leaves the tab order, so focus can't land on something invisible.
- **Next up:**
  - When the trailer ends, the card's **Play now** and **Hide** are reachable, and the countdown is announced once.
  - Hide stops the countdown and moves focus to **Replay**.
- **Behind the scenes, View all:**
  - A modal dialog with In scene, Skills, Facts and Trivia tabs (arrow keys), and Close.
  - It pauses the trailer, and Esc closes the panel, not the player. Focus returns to View all.

### Search page

The field, **Clear**, then the result cards. With JavaScript it narrows as you type, and the result count is announced politely.

### Categories, skill and collection pages

The genre tiles and the featured collection tiles, then on a skill page: the Categories link, Titles, Certifications, Episodes and More categories.

### Career Guide

1. **Earlier**, **Now** and **Later** scroll the guide six months at a time. No dragging is needed.
2. The ON NOW card, then every programme block, one stop each, in channel order.
   - Focusing a block scrolls it into view.
   - A short block widens on focus to show its whole name.
3. It's a real table: career tracks are row headers and months are column headers, so a screen reader announces each block's channel and month.

### Contact

1. **Email Bhavini** (white CTA) and **Download resume**.
2. Each channel's link, then **Copy email address** and **Copy phone number**.
   - The button says "Copied" (or "Couldn't copy") and a polite status repeats it.
3. Open profile and Visit f1jobs.io, both marked "(opens in a new tab)", then Download.

### My List

Download resume, the saved titles (newest first), or the empty state's Browse all titles. Every "+" on the site announces "Added … to My List" or "Removed … from My List" politely.

## Reduced motion

- **Off entirely:**
  - The ident is skipped, sound included.
  - The hero carousel doesn't auto-advance and its push-in stops.
  - Scrolling into a title page doesn't start the trailer.
  - The watch page doesn't autoplay; it waits on Play.
- **Instant instead of animated:**
  - Card and hover-card scaling.
  - Fades and row scrolling.
  - The guide's scroll buttons and the search card hover.

## Without JavaScript

- **Pages:**
  - Every route renders its heading and content.
  - Rows are plain lists of links.
  - The title tabs show all three sections, each with its own heading.
  - `/search/` lists everything.
  - The guide is a static table.
- **Contact details:**
  - The email and phone are spelled out ("… at gmail dot com") rather than linked.
  - The resume always downloads.
- **Interactive pieces:**
  - The picker never opens.
  - The search popover still opens (a native popover) and submits to `/search/`.

## Notes and known limits

- **The trailers are silent motion graphics.** Each has a WebVTT captions track with every word it shows: a text alternative for video-only content (1.2.1). The volume control says so rather than pretending to work.
- **The Behind the scenes quick view isn't a live region.** Announcing every scene change would talk over everything. The same content is one keystroke away in View all.
- **Year-only dates (e.g. "2023 – 2026") span their whole years in the Career Guide.** The block's label always shows the dates as written.
- **Prime's own player chrome is undocumented for keyboard users** (research/03-uiux.md, [U]). The shortcuts here follow common web-player conventions.
